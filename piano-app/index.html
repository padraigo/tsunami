How it works & tips
  • Listen plays the piece with a synthesized piano while highlighting each note in time with the beat.
  • Practice gives a one-bar count-in, then listens through your microphone while you play. It grades each note by pitch and shows your accuracy. Run it on http://localhost or HTTPS so the browser allows microphone access.
  • Use the Tempo slider to slow tricky passages down — the note highlighting and metronome follow the BPM exactly.
  • Supports whole, half, quarter, eighth and sixteenth notes, dotted notes, rests, accidentals and time signatures.
